HOW TO PLAY

Swipe a row or column to line up 3+ matching tiles.
Dragging to look around is free — you only pay ⚡ energy for your final move. Sit idle too long, though, and the reactor drains on its own.
Match Electric (⚡) tiles to refill your energy — that's your main power source.
Chain cascades for huge multipliers, and discover elemental combinations for special effects!
Out of energy for any move? You get a board reshuffle (limited per level), then one last free move — miss both and it's shutdown.
